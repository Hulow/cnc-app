# About Us

## and our 3-axis CNC milling machine

Your CNC service in Berlin. We offer computer-controlled 2D cutting of plates or sheets, 3D form milling, and milling of 3D surfaces, as well as all-around machining of 3D objects.

This includes cutting out letters and lettering, creating reliefs, individual jewelry pieces, awards, sculptures, pedestals, engravings, and panels with 3D surfaces for use as cabinet doors, wall panels, and inlays.
We also offer 3D precision parts made from solid aluminum, copper, brass, carbon fiber, resin, plastic, model-making foam, and composite materials.
Individual workpieces can be up to 1100mm x 720mm x 250mm. Larger objects can be processed upon request.

3D milling is used in model making, prototype construction, trade fair construction, furniture making, and advertising signs. In the manufacture of door handles, tool handles, attachments for special equipment, housing construction, shells and formwork, as well as molds and casting forms.

For designers, model makers, hobbyists, students and you.
